What is ComfyUI, and why is it hard to share?

  • The open-source standard for AI images and video. Netflix, Apple, Nike use it.
  • Paid tools: $79–215 a seat, your data in their cloud.
  • A desktop app: no login, add-ons run any code.
  • So teams give everyone a GPU, idle most of the day.

So what's the design?

  • Same ComfyUI, same canvas. Only the GPUs move.
  • Every Run becomes a job on one queue.
  • A pool of GPUs takes the jobs. Idle? Zero machines.
  • Models download once, to shared storage.

ComfyUI runs any code. How is sharing safe?

  • GPU workers are unreachable, not defended.
  • No network address. The only way in is the queue.
  • No internet out: models come in through the gateway, checked.
  • Company SSO at the door. GPU time metered per person.

What does it cost, and what's the catch?

  • Ten designers: ~$7,100/mo as a GPU each, $120–950 as a pool.
  • Works because few people hit Run at once.
  • Catch: 8–17 min cold start. One warm GPU fixes it, ~$190/mo.
  • Catch: new add-on code means an image rebuild.

Why not serve each model on vLLM?

  • vLLM keeps a model loaded on a GPU. Best for one busy model.
  • A design job is a graph: 2–3 models, many non-model steps.
  • Each extra model on vLLM: +$713/mo. In the pool: disk.
  • Use both: move a model to vLLM Omni once it dominates usage.

Where is it, and what do we need?

  • Built: ~9,000 lines of wiring. OpenShift already had the parts.
  • Ran end to end on real GPUs. Also runs on a laptop.
  • Not proven at scale: a handful of users, three workers.
  • Ask: an owner, a 30-minute PM read, ~$50 for a cluster day.
